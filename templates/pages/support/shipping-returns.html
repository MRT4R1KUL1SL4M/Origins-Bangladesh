Support Services

Shipping & Returns

Clear, reliable delivery and simple return guidance—so you can shop our heritage collections with absolute confidence. We ensure your treasures reach you safely and offer hassle-free resolutions if they don't perfectly meet your expectations.

Premium Shipping

  • Standard delivery typically takes 3-5 business days, while custom or artisan-crafted items require specialized lead times.
  • Real-time tracking updates via email and SMS, ensuring you are informed at every step of your order's journey.
  • Each piece is meticulously packed using premium, eco-friendly materials designed to protect delicate artisan work in transit.
  • We collaborate exclusively with top-tier logistics partners experienced in handling high-value heritage goods.

Returns & Refunds

  • We gladly accept returns within 7 days of delivery for eligible items kept in their original, unused condition with all tags attached.
  • Report any transit damage or discrepancies immediately upon delivery to trigger our priority resolution process.
  • Refunds are processed securely to your original payment method within 5-7 business days following our quality inspection.
  • A dedicated support concierge will be assigned to assist you seamlessly through the entire return or exchange process.

Our dedicated support concierge is ready to guide you step-by-step based on your specific order details.
